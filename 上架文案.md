## 描述 Description

```
Your car's whole story, on your phone.

When did you last change the oil? What has this car really cost you this year? Most of us have no idea — the receipts pile up, the reminders get forgotten, and the apps that promise to help want a login and lose your data after an update.

Garagely is different. Add every vehicle, log a service in seconds, and let the app remember what's due — by date OR by mileage. Everything lives on your device. No account, no sign-in, no ads, ever.

— FREE, FOREVER —
• Log service, repairs, fuel-ups and expenses — with photos & receipts
• Reminders by date AND mileage — Garagely predicts when you'll hit a mileage and reminds you in time
• Log a service and the matching reminder reschedules itself automatically
• Automatic MPG (and km/L) between fill-ups
• Full EV & plug-in support — kWh, charge cost, mi/kWh, home vs public, battery health
• Total cost of ownership — fuel, charging, service and expenses in one number
• A Specs card for filter numbers, bulb sizes, tire size & PSI — handy at the parts counter
• A glovebox for insurance, registration & warranty documents — with expiry reminders
• Home Screen, Lock Screen & StandBy widgets, plus "What's my next service?" with Siri
• 100% on-device. No account, no bank connection, no tracking

— GARAGELY PRO (one-time, $5.99) —
Pay once. Yours forever. No subscription.
• Unlimited vehicles — cars, motorcycles, RVs, no limit
• Full analytics — cost-per-mile, economy trends, yearly totals
• Export CSV & PDF — a complete history to hand the next owner
• iCloud sync — your garage on every device
• Service templates — reusable schedules, one tap to apply
• Six alternate app icons

— WHY GARAGELY —
The big car apps want your account, bury a tracker inside a used-car marketplace, and have been known to wipe years of records in a bad update. Garagely is the opposite: everything is yours, on your device, bought once — and your history can never be paywalled or logged out from under you.

Stop guessing what your car needs and what it costs. Start with Garagely.

On-device & private. No account, ever.
```
