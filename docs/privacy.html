Garagely

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Garagely is private by design. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account system and no way for us to see your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Garagely does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no sign-up and no login.

Where your data lives

Your vehicles, service records, fuel and charging logs, expenses, reminders, photos and documents are stored locally on your iPhone. Your next-service summary is shared with the app's own widgets through Apple's on-device App Group storage.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable iCloud Sync (a Garagely Pro feature), your garage is synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit, so it appears on your other devices. This data lives in your iCloud and is never accessible to us.

Photos & camera

When you attach a photo or document to a record or to your glovebox, it is stored locally with that item. Camera and photo-library access are used only for this purpose, only when you choose to add a file.

Notifications

Service and document reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Apple's local notification system. No notification content is sent to any server.

Purchases

Garagely Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see your payment details.

Analytics & tracking

None. Garagely contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children

Garagely is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
