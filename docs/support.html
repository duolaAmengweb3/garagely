Support

Garagely is the simple, private way to track your car's upkeep — on your device, no account. Here are answers to common questions. Still stuck? We reply to every email.

Do I need an account?

No. Garagely never asks you to sign in. Everything is stored locally on your iPhone.

Will reminders actually notify me?

Yes — date reminders fire as local notifications. For mileage-based reminders, Garagely estimates when you'll reach that mileage from your driving history and notifies you ahead of time. Make sure notifications are enabled in Settings.

How is the next service reminder rescheduled?

When you log a service that matches a recurring reminder, Garagely offers to mark it done and automatically schedule the next one from your interval.

Does it support electric vehicles?

Fully. Set a vehicle's powertrain to Electric or Plug-in Hybrid to log charging in kWh, see mi/kWh efficiency, track home vs public charging, and record battery health over time.

What's in Garagely Pro?

A one-time $5.99 unlock: unlimited vehicles, full cost-per-mile analytics, CSV & PDF export, iCloud sync, service templates, and alternate app icons. No subscription.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Enable iCloud Sync (Pro) to have your garage appear on every device, or export a CSV/PDF of any vehicle's full history at any time.

Is my data safe if I delete the app?

Deleting the app removes its local data. Export a CSV/PDF first, or turn on iCloud Sync, if you want to keep a copy.
